### 🏛️ Engineering & Craftsmanship Highlights

#### 1. 🌐 Real-Time 3D WebGL & Ember Particle Simulation
* **Ember Physics Engine:** GPU-accelerated particle buffer geometry simulating glowing embers rising from live-fire hearths, with randomized velocity vectors and opacity oscillations.
* **Abstract 3D Torus Knot Mesh:** High-precision metallic PBR mesh with dynamic point lights responding in real time to user scroll progress and mouse coordinates.
* **Smooth Camera Kinematics:** Frame-interpolated 3D viewport camera transitions that react smoothly across all desktop and mobile viewports.

#### 2. 💎 Apple Clear Liquid Glass Design System
* **Backdrop Filter Compositing:** Multi-layer frosted glass cards with specular gloss highlights (`backdrop-filter: blur(22px) saturate(125%)`).
* **Micro-Interactions:** Fluid hover lifts, soft ember glow borders, and elegant typography blending Fraunces serif with Archivo.
* **Warm Amber Lighting Aesthetic:** Evokes the warmth of live-fire coals, wood-fired hearths, and intimate boutique hospitality.

#### 3. 🛎️ Curated Hospitality Suite & Dining
* **Five Curated Rooms Upstairs:** Interactive visual showcase of The Hearth Room, The Copper Suite, The Ember Loft, The Smoke House, and The Lantern Room.
* **Live-Fire Kitchen Dining:** Interactive course tabs for Fire Starters, From the Coals, Embers & Sweets, and The Cellar.
* **Interactive Reviews & Location:** Guest testimonial cards, Google Maps integration, and direct reservation inquiries.
