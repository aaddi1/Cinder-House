Behind the pass, most nights of the week.

“I don't season food to impress you. I season it the way my mother taught me — enough salt to remember it by morning.”

Rehan Qureshi opened Cinder House in a converted print shop with one grill and eleven seats. Fourteen years later the grill is bigger, the seats are the same number, and the rooms upstairs came from turning the storage floor into somewhere his regulars could sleep off a long dinner. He still buys the coal himself, every Tuesday, from the same supplier as always.

— Rehan Qureshi, Owner & Head Chef
